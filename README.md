# ResolveIQ

## Managed Renting, Made Simpler

ResolveIQ is a managed rental platform designed to make renting easier for tenants and less demanding for landlords.

We address two common problems in the Philippine rental market:

**For tenants:** High upfront move-in costs, large security deposits, post-dated cheque requirements, and complicated application processes.

**For landlords:** Tenant screening, payment collection, follow-ups, documentation, maintenance coordination, and ongoing tenancy administration.

ResolveIQ sits between the tenant and landlord to manage the rental journey—from property discovery and tenant verification through payments and ongoing tenancy management.

> **We verify. Landlords decide. We manage the process.**

---

## The Problem

Renting a property can require a significant amount of cash before a tenant even moves in.

A property renting for ₱20,000 per month may require:

- 2 months security deposit — ₱40,000
- 1 month advance — ₱20,000
- Post-dated cheques
- Supporting documents

That's **₱60,000 upfront**, before moving expenses and other costs.

For qualified tenants, affordability isn't always the problem.

Sometimes the problem is **timing**.

At the same time, landlords require deposits, post-dated cheques, and other protections for legitimate reasons. They need confidence that rent will be paid, their property will be protected, and problems will be addressed.

ResolveIQ is designed to help both sides meet in the middle.

# How ResolveIQ Works

## For Tenants

### 1. Discover

Browse available rental properties.

Property listings provide important information including rental price, location, property details, move-in requirements, and available rental terms.

### 2. Schedule a Viewing

Interested tenants request a property viewing.

Viewing arrangements are coordinated with the landlord or authorized property representative.

### 3. View the Property

The tenant views the property and decides whether they want to proceed.

There is no obligation to apply simply because a viewing was scheduled.

### 4. Intent to Apply

If the tenant wants the property, they submit an Intent to Apply.

At this stage, ResolveIQ collects basic information needed for pre-qualification, including the tenant's preferred move-in date.

### 5. Free Pre-Qualification

ResolveIQ performs an initial affordability and eligibility assessment.

This is designed to determine whether it makes sense for the tenant, landlord, and ResolveIQ to proceed to formal verification.

**There is no verification fee at this stage.**

### 6. Landlord Confirmation

Before the tenant pays for formal verification, ResolveIQ contacts the landlord.

The landlord is informed that a prospective tenant has expressed interest and is provided with the proposed rental structure and intended move-in date.

The landlord confirms whether they are willing to proceed with the applicant under those proposed terms.

This helps prevent tenants from paying for verification when the landlord is not willing to consider the proposed arrangement.

### 7. Paid Verification

Once the landlord agrees to proceed, the tenant is invited to complete formal verification.

The verification process may include:

- Identity verification
- Government-issued identification
- Employment verification
- Income verification
- Proof of income
- Affordability assessment
- Previous landlord/reference verification
- Credit information, where legally permitted and with appropriate consent
- Document consistency checks
- Other appropriate tenancy screening

A verification processing fee applies.

The exact requirements may vary depending on the applicant and property.

**Payment for verification does not guarantee approval of the rental application.**

### 8. Verification Outcome

Once verification is completed, ResolveIQ determines whether the applicant meets the applicable qualification requirements.

Possible outcomes may include:

**Qualified**

The applicant meets the requirements to proceed.

**Additional Review Required**

Additional information or documentation is required before a determination can be made.

**Not Qualified**

The applicant does not currently meet the requirements for the proposed rental arrangement.

ResolveIQ communicates the outcome to the tenant.

Relevant verification results are also communicated to the landlord in accordance with applicable privacy and consent requirements.

### 9. Final Landlord Approval

The landlord makes the final decision on whether to accept the tenant.

ResolveIQ facilitates the decision but does not replace the landlord's authority over their property.

> **We verify. The landlord decides.**

### 10. Confirm Move-In Terms

Once approved, ResolveIQ confirms:

- Monthly rental amount
- Move-in date
- Advance rent
- Initial security deposit
- Remaining security deposit schedule, if applicable
- Payment schedule
- Lease duration
- Other agreed tenancy conditions

Everything is documented before funds are collected.

### 11. Move-In Payment

The tenant pays the agreed move-in amount through the approved ResolveIQ payment process.

Depending on the property and qualification outcome, eligible tenants may be offered a structured security deposit arrangement.

For example:

Traditional requirement:

**2 months deposit + 1 month advance**

Alternative qualified arrangement:

**1 month advance + partial security deposit upfront**

with the remaining security deposit accumulated according to an agreed schedule.

Flexible deposit arrangements are subject to landlord approval and tenant qualification.

### 12. Agreements & Documentation

ResolveIQ coordinates the required documentation.

The primary lease remains between the:

**Landlord ↔ Tenant**

Additional agreements may define ResolveIQ's role in payment administration and tenancy management.

Where appropriate, agreements may be notarized.

### 13. Move In

Once:

- Required payments have cleared
- Agreements have been signed
- Required documentation is complete
- Move-in conditions have been satisfied

the tenant receives access to the property.

# After Move-In

ResolveIQ doesn't disappear after the lease is signed.

That is where the managed rental experience begins.

## Payment Management

Tenants make rental payments through the designated ResolveIQ payment process.

ResolveIQ maintains payment records and coordinates remittance according to the landlord agreement.

> **Every peso has a record.**

Tenants receive clear records of payments made.

Landlords receive visibility into rental collections and payment status.

## Payment Reminders

ResolveIQ manages routine payment communication, including:

- Upcoming payment reminders
- Due-date notifications
- Payment confirmation
- Overdue follow-up
- Payment documentation

This reduces the need for landlords to repeatedly contact tenants regarding rent.

## Maintenance Coordination

Tenants can report property issues through ResolveIQ.

ResolveIQ documents the concern and coordinates communication with the landlord and appropriate service providers.

The landlord remains responsible for decisions requiring owner authorization unless otherwise agreed.

## Tenancy Records

ResolveIQ maintains relevant tenancy records including:

- Rental payments
- Deposit payments
- Agreements
- Maintenance requests
- Tenant-landlord communications handled through the platform
- Important tenancy events

This creates a clearer record for both parties.

# Flexible Security Deposits

ResolveIQ's goal is **not to eliminate landlord protection**.

It is to explore whether qualified tenants can build part of their security deposit over time instead of funding the entire amount before moving in.

For example, a property renting for ₱20,000 may traditionally require:

| Requirement | Traditional |
|---|---:|
| 2 Months Security Deposit | ₱40,000 |
| 1 Month Advance | ₱20,000 |
| **Initial Requirement** | **₱60,000** |

An approved flexible arrangement could instead begin with:

| Requirement | Example Flexible Arrangement |
|---|---:|
| Initial Security Deposit | ₱10,000 |
| 1 Month Advance | ₱20,000 |
| **Initial Requirement** | **₱30,000** |

The remaining security deposit could then be accumulated according to an agreed schedule.

This is an example only.

Actual terms depend on:

- Property
- Landlord requirements
- Tenant qualification
- Verification results
- Agreed payment schedule

Landlords may choose to offer qualified tenants alternative deposit arrangements designed to reduce move-in barriers while maintaining structured protections.

# What ResolveIQ Does Not Do

Transparency matters.

ResolveIQ is a rental management and facilitation platform.

Unless expressly stated in a separate agreement:

**ResolveIQ does not guarantee tenant rent payments.**

If a tenant fails to pay rent, ResolveIQ manages the agreed collection and follow-up process, but the underlying payment obligation remains with the tenant.

ResolveIQ also does not guarantee that:

- Every applicant will qualify
- Every landlord will accept flexible deposit arrangements
- Every property will offer the same payment structure

Terms are property-specific and subject to approval.

# Our Decision Framework

ResolveIQ is being built around a simple principle:

**Start with the outcome—not the technology.**

The desired outcome is:

> Help qualified tenants access suitable homes with more manageable move-in costs while giving landlords appropriate visibility, control, and protection.

From there, ResolveIQ determines:

**Outcome → Decision → Verification → Intervention → Human Oversight → Measurement → Learning**

During the initial stage of the platform, tenant qualification is intentionally based on documented criteria and human review.

We are not pretending that a new company with limited historical data can accurately predict tenant behavior using an artificial intelligence risk score.

Instead, ResolveIQ will build its decision framework using real tenancy outcomes over time.

# Privacy & Consent

Tenant information is sensitive.

ResolveIQ is committed to collecting only information reasonably necessary for tenant verification and tenancy management.

Personal information must be handled in accordance with applicable Philippine privacy requirements.

Where third-party verification, credit information, or other sensitive information is required, appropriate disclosure and consent procedures will be followed.

Landlords receive information relevant to their rental decision rather than unrestricted access to an applicant's personal records.
