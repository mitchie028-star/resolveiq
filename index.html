The operating loop

Detect, decide, act, validate, improve.

ResolvedIQ works like an operating system for ecommerce CX. It watches for customer risk, applies merchant playbooks, takes safe actions, pauses risky cases for review, and shows what to fix next.

1

Detect

Identify shipping, refund, tracking, and customer-risk signals early.

2

Decide

Apply your support, refund, exchange, and escalation policies.

3

Act

Resolve safe cases automatically or route judgment calls to humans.

4

Validate

QA checks every action for policy compliance, tone, and risk.

5

Improve

See where your CX operation, policies, and customer expectations can improve.
