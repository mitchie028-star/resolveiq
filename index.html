The rental problem

Renting shouldn't be harder than finding the right home.

The traditional rental process can create friction on both sides. ResolvedIQ is being designed to create a better middle ground.

01 / TENANTS

Too much cash upfront.

Deposits, advance rent, post-dated checks and paperwork can make moving expensive — even for tenants who can comfortably afford the monthly rent.

02 / LANDLORDS

Too much work after listing.

Screening applicants, collecting rent, following up, maintaining records and coordinating tenant concerns all take time.

How it works

From finding a home to moving in.

A structured rental journey designed to make the process clearer for tenants while keeping the landlord in control of the final tenancy decision.

01

Find & view

Browse available homes, select a property and schedule a viewing before deciding whether you want to apply.

02

Pre-qualify

Interested in the property? Complete an initial eligibility and affordability check.

03

Landlord confirmation

Before formal verification begins, we confirm that the landlord wants to proceed with the prospective tenant and proposed rental arrangement.

04

Paid verification

Once the landlord confirms, the tenant can proceed with the applicable verification process and pay the disclosed verification or processing fee.

05

Verification & results

Required identity, employment, income, affordability and other applicable checks are completed. Relevant results are then communicated to the tenant and landlord.

06

Landlord decision

The landlord reviews the relevant outcome and retains final approval of the tenancy.

07

Confirm move-in

Once approved, we confirm the tenant's intended move-in date and the agreed move-in requirements.

08

Payment & paperwork

The tenant completes the agreed move-in payment and the required rental documentation is prepared and completed.

For tenants

Your deposit shouldn't stand between you and your next home.

For participating properties, qualified tenants may be considered for a structured security deposit arrangement where part of the deposit is completed over an agreed period rather than paid entirely before move-in.

Better access for tenants shouldn't mean removing landlord protection. ResolvedIQ is being designed around verification, structured processes, human review and clearer records so both sides can make better-informed rental decisions.
