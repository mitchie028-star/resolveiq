How it works

Detect → Decide → Resolve → Escalate → Improve

ResolveIQ identifies repetitive support workflows, applies your CX policies, resolves safe tickets automatically, escalates sensitive situations, and shows what is driving support volume.

1

Detect

2

Decide

3

Resolve

4
